Oracle gains versus predicted-attribute utility in Bangla writer identification. Points give equally weighted mean top-1 changes over 120 settings and 42 writers; bars are conditional 95% hand-stratified writer-bootstrap intervals. Known query handedness and the expected known balanced random partition are oracle controls. Fixed fusion, nested learned fusion and hard filtering use predicted query handedness and known gallery hands. The study uses one controlled cohort; intervals do not include new-gallery or full model-refitting uncertainty.
