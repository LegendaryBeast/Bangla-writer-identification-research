## Evidentiary boundaries

CSAFE is external-dataset evaluation with within-dataset refitting, not transfer of a Bangla-trained predictor. Its same-prompt condition and cross-prompt condition must be distinguished; session 3 and alternate predictors are predefined secondary checks. Individual intervals are conditional and not multiplicity-adjusted; they are not inversions of the discrete sign-flip tests. Overlapping galleries are dependent sensitivity analyses. Local specification of the CSAFE contrasts is not a globally registered external-dataset sampling plan. The below-chance adjusted predictor is reported without assigning a causal acquisition mechanism. Numerical optimizer certification is distinct from predictive calibration quality.
